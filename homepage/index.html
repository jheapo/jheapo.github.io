Home page variants

Six versions of the same new home page. They differ in two ways:

  • Width (D21). 1: everything in one 1,120 px column. 2: text in a reading column, screenshots in a wider band up to 1,400 px. 3: as 2, and clicking a screenshot opens it at full size.
  • Phone menus (D23). A: Products and Use cases open inside the phone menu. B: Products and Use cases are links to their sections on the page. Narrow the window to below 860 px to see the difference.
