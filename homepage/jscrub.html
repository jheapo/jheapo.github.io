JScrub — Safely Share Heap Dumps

A heap dump is a full copy of your application's memory — personal data and all. JScrub anonymizes it so 3rd parties can analyze the heap without being exposed to private data.

No inference

JScrub anonymizes class, field and method names, not just the values — so the dump gives away neither data nor its meaning.

Your data never leaves home

Anonymization runs on your own infrastructure; the raw dump never leaves it. Personal data stays with you and you alone.

You hold the key

JScrub can re-identify a JHeapo report back to real values for readability — but the key stays with you.
