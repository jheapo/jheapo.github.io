Why it matters

Wasted memory is not free. Every duplicated string and half-empty collection makes the JVM work harder. Cut the waste and three things improve at once.

Lower latency

A smaller live set means less to allocate, scan, and copy. Requests spend less time waiting on the JVM.

Stable latency

Less garbage means fewer and shorter GC pauses — flatter tail latencies and fewer nasty p99 spikes.

Lower cost

Fit in a smaller heap and you fit on smaller instances, or pack more per node. Wasted RAM is a line item.

Often it takes changing only a handful of lines to reclaim a large fraction of the heap. JHeapo points you straight at them.
