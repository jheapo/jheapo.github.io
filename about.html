About Us

Three tools, one goal — and the experience behind them.

GCSee, JHeapo, and JScrub are three tools with a single goal: help you understand and shrink your Java application's memory — from GC logs to heap dumps — and do it safely.

They grew out of years of diagnosing Java memory problems in production: reading GC logs to find what to tune, cracking open heap dumps too big for the usual tools, and doing it without ever exposing the private data a real heap contains.

The tools are only half the story. When you'd rather hand the problem to someone who does this every day, our analysis service turns your pseudonymized data into concrete findings and recommendations.
